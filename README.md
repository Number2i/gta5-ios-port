## 🗂️ Preparing Your Own Game Files

This project does not include any game files, so you need a prepared copy of the game on your PC first. If your copy of GTA V is installed through a launcher or storefront, keep it installed and updated on the Windows machine while you work through these steps.

What "prepared" means in practice:

1. Your GTA V installation is complete and playable on the PC.
2. The game has been launched at least once so all support folders and settings exist.
3. You know the exact folder where the game data lives — usually inside your launcher's library folder.
4. You have made a backup copy of that folder in case you want to start over.

Copy, do not move, your game data into the project folder you created in the previous section. Keeping the original installation intact means you can still play on your PC and can re-copy clean files any time something goes wrong.

## 📱 Moving Files to Your iPhone

After the project files and your game data are sitting together on the PC, the next stage is getting them onto the phone.

1. Connect your iPhone to the Windows computer with the cable.
2. Unlock the phone and tap Trust when the prompt appears.
3. Open iTunes or the Apple Devices app on the PC and wait for the phone to appear.
4. Use the file sharing area for the project's app to add the prepared folder.
5. Leave the phone connected and awake until the transfer finishes — large game files take a while.
6. Disconnect only after the file list shows the full transfer is complete.

If the transfer stalls at any point, unplug the cable, restart both the phone and the computer, and try again. Interrupting a partial transfer is the most frequent cause of a black screen on first launch.

## ▶️ First Launch

With the files in place, open the app on your iPhone from the home screen. The very first start takes noticeably longer than later ones because the project has to read through your game data and build the files it needs.

Things to expect on the first run:

- A longer loading period, sometimes several minutes.
- A brief black screen while assets are indexed.
- A settings screen where you can lower graphics quality before the world loads.

Start with lower graphics settings, then raise them once you know the phone handles it comfortably. Higher settings look better but drain the battery faster and can cause stutter on older iPhones.

## ⚙️ Tips for Smooth Performance

Getting a steady experience is mostly about a few small settings rather than anything technical.

- Lower the resolution scale first if the game stutters — it is the single biggest performance lever.
- Reduce draw distance and shadow quality on older phones.
- Close background apps before playing, especially camera and map apps.
- Play with the phone plugged in or on a cool surface during long sessions.
- Turn off low power mode, which can throttle the phone mid-game.
- Keep at least 10 GB of free phone storage so the game has room to work.

## 🧰 Troubleshooting Common Problems

**The app closes as soon as it opens.** Your game files are likely incomplete or the transfer was interrupted. Re-copy the game data from your PC and redo the file transfer.

**The screen stays black for a long time.** This is normal on the first launch. Wait several minutes before assuming something is wrong.

**The game runs but stutters constantly.** Drop the graphics preset one notch and turn off shadows. Check that low power mode is disabled.

**Files do not appear in the file sharing area.** Update iTunes or the Apple Devices app, reconnect the cable, and confirm you tapped Trust on the phone.

**The phone gets very warm.** Long sessions heat any phone. Remove the case, avoid direct sunlight, and take short breaks.

**A newer version appears on the project page.** Download it, keep your game files untouched, and replace only the project files.
